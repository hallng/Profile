About

Welcome to my faculty homepage

Nicholas G. Hall is Berry Family Professor of Operations Management in the Department of Operations and Business Analytics at Fisher College of Business, The Ohio State University, with a courtesy professorship in the Department of Integrated Systems Engineering.

His research addresses scheduling, project management, behavioral issues in operations, tournament design for sports, and applications of operations research. He has published 104 refereed papers in leading journals, including Operations Research, Management Science, Mathematics of Operations Research, Manufacturing & Service Operations Management, and INFORMS Journal on Computing. A 2021 bibliometric study ranked him first among all researchers for publications in scheduling.

He is co-author, with Z.-L. Chen, of Supply Chain Scheduling (Springer, 2022, 706 pages). His Google Scholar citations exceed 8,300, with an h-index of 41. He has given 460 presentations in 30 countries, including 220 invited talks, 20 keynote addresses, and 10 INFORMS tutorials.

He teaches graduate M.B.A. courses in project management and sports analytics, as well as executive education short courses. His award-winning project management course has been adopted by 25 universities in 9 countries. He served as President of INFORMS in 2018, where he established the society's government advocacy program, and was elected a Fellow of INFORMS in 2021 — a distinction limited to 2% of the membership.

Research

Four principal research areas

Project Management

Optimization for planning and execution

Application of modern optimization and robust optimization methods to address the main planning and execution decisions within project frameworks.

Supply Chain Scheduling

Coordination across decision makers

Coordination of scheduling decisions across multiple functions and among self-interested decision makers, to improve overall supply chain performance.

Behavioral Operations

Present bias and incentive design

The effect of present bias on operational decision making, and the design of incentives to mitigate its effects on operational performance.

Sports Analytics

Tournament design

Design of tournaments to improve competitive outcomes and to reduce anti-competitive behavior such as shirking and strategic opponent choice.

Bibliometric standing

Ranked 1st for publications in the research field of scheduling in Operations Research over the period 1952–2019, with 8 of the 128 articles in the h-index of Operations Research.

International engagement

Visiting professorships at universities in Australia, China, Hong Kong, Japan, Singapore, and the United Kingdom; 460 presentations delivered across 30 countries.

Applied research

Consulting and applied projects spanning healthcare optimization at the National Cancer Institute, financial systems at BancOne, and manufacturing process improvement for O.M. Scott & Sons.

    Teaching

    Prescriptive analytics, taught by discussion

    Professor Hall's teaching emphasizes prescriptive analytics — helping students determine the best course of action.
